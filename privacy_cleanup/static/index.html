More options you can use directly

You do not need this tool to take these steps. Availability and requirements vary by service and location.

  • PeopleConnect suppression center ↗

    Free, user-operated suppression of name-search reports on TruthFinder, Intelius, Instant Checkmate and US Search. Email verification is required; suppression does not erase source records or cover every type of search.

  • Official California data broker registry ↗

    Find broker websites and privacy-request contacts for direct requests. The registry lists businesses; it does not tell you which ones hold your information or guarantee eligibility outside California.

  • Google Results about you ↗

    Find eligible Google results containing your personal information and request their removal from Search. Removing a search result leaves the information on the original website. Feature availability varies.
