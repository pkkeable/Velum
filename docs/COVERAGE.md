# Coverage and evidence

Five email contracts exist at **automated simulation**. No route is pilot-tested
or maintained live coverage. A simulated provider receipt proves only the local
execution path. Source dates and the complete templates live in
[`contracts.py`](../privacy_cleanup/contracts.py); official links are below.

| Route | Encoded scope | Official source | Remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apollo deletion | Policy-based name/email request, no encoded residence restriction | [Privacy policy](https://www.apollo.io/privacy-policy) | Broker acceptance, matching and verification untested |
| Cognism deletion | CA, CO, CT, FL, MT, OR, TX, VA, UT | [US notice](https://www.cognism.com/en/us-privacy-policy) | Only named states encoded; actual processing untested |
| Lusha erasure | California | [Privacy notice](https://www.lusha.com/legal/privacy-notice/) | Other residence channels not encoded |
| ContactOut erasure | General rights section without an explicit residency condition | [Data-subject policy](https://contactout.com/privacy_data_subjects) | This reading does not establish worldwide entitlement or accepted removal |
| Hunter deletion | 15 named US states; California excluded; owner-marked professional email | [Policy](https://hunter.io/privacy-policy), [CA notice](https://hunter.io/privacy-policy/california) | Provisional: actual email processing versus redirection to the Claim page is untested |

The contracts were checked October 8–9, 2026. Their 30-day freshness policy pauses
individual expired routes; dates must not be advanced without re-reading the source.
Do not infer live permission from code presence or a shared privacy address.

Epsilon has a local US manual-preparation flow. PeopleConnect, Acxiom, Whitepages,
PeopleFinders, Spokeo and BeenVerified have incomplete guidance/tracking evidence.
TruePeopleSearch, FastPeopleSearch and Nuwber remain research candidates. No suitable
automatic household route is established. Challenges/blocked retrieval and explicit
automation restrictions are different evidence; neither authorizes a bypass.

California residents can use [official DROP](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/) directly.
Velum neither transfers identity to it nor claims its coverage as Velum automation.
Non-California users retain whatever individual routes their own information qualifies
for. User-reported external requests participate in duplicate/overlap review.
